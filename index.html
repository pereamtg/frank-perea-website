Questions Before You Call

Does this cost anything?+
No. The consultation, the numbers review, and the credit analysis are free.
Do I really need 20% down?+
Usually not. We'll tell you the real number for your situation.
What kind of loans do you offer?+
FHA, VA, Conventional, USDA, and First-Time Homebuyer Down Payment Assistance programs. Most buyers don't call already knowing which one they need — figuring out which program fits is part of your free numbers review.
What if my credit isn't great?+
On your free numbers review we'll talk through your credit range — no pull required. If you apply, we'll run a full Credit Expert Analysis on your actual report with specific next steps.
How long does this take?+
Contract to closing usually takes about 30 days. Start to finish, 60 to 90 days.
What if I'm not ready yet?+
We'll give you a Homebuyer Readiness Roadmap and a specific time to check back in.
Do you work in Spanish?+
Yes. Many of the families we work with prefer Spanish, and our team can help in both languages. Hablamos español.
