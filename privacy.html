Perea Mortgage – Privacy Policy

Perea Mortgage is a branch/DBA of Canopy Mortgage, LLC (NMLS #1359687). All loans are originated directly by Canopy Mortgage, LLC.

This page explains how Perea Mortgage collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us through pereamortgage.com, our booking calendar, our homebuyer quiz, and our text messages.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

No mobile information or SMS opt-in consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messages come directly from Perea Mortgage. Your phone number and SMS consent are never sold, rented, or transferred to third-party lenders, lead buyers, or outside investors.

We only use your mobile number to send the messages you asked for, such as appointment confirmations and reminders, document requests, and loan status updates. You can stop at any time by replying STOP. See our SMS Terms for details.

Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, and answers you provide when you book a call, take our homebuyer quiz, or contact us.
  • Loan application information: if you apply for a mortgage, your application is handled by Canopy Mortgage, LLC and is covered by the Canopy Mortgage Privacy Policy linked below.
  • Website usage: we use Google Analytics to see how visitors use our site (for example, which pages are viewed). This uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.

How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and confirm your appointments
  • To answer your questions and provide mortgage guidance
  • To process your loan application through Canopy Mortgage, LLC
  • To send you the emails and text messages you agreed to receive
  • To improve our website

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to serve you, for example with Canopy Mortgage, LLC to process your loan, with service providers that help us run our scheduling and messaging tools, or when required by law. These providers may not use your information for their own marketing.

Your Choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop texts.
  • Click "unsubscribe" in any email to stop marketing emails.
  • Contact us to update your information or ask questions about it.

Canopy Mortgage Privacy Policy

Because Perea Mortgage is a branch of Canopy Mortgage, LLC, the Canopy Mortgage Privacy Policy also applies, including your rights under federal privacy law:
