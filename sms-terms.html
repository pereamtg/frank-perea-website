SMS Messaging Program

Perea Mortgage provides non-promotional SMS messaging to customers who have requested mortgage-related assistance and consented to receive text messages.

Messages may include appointment confirmations and reminders, document requests, mortgage application status updates, and customer service communications.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your appointments, mortgage application activity, and customer service needs.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-In and Consent

Customers may consent to receive SMS messages by checking the optional SMS consent checkbox on our website booking form.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not required to book an appointment or obtain mortgage services.

Opt-Out Instructions

You can cancel SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any text message from us. Once your opt-out request is processed, you will no longer receive messages through this SMS program unless you opt in again.
